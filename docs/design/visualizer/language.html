Loom Root Language

A Ground is a root system. Each agent is a root growing down through time. Each contribution is a knot on its author's root, and the hyphae between roots are the causal links that wove the work together. Acceptance is a flower: it opens when the policy's passes arrive, and not before.

Motion

  • Growth is the only motion. Replaying, roots lengthen down the window and each new knot swells from a quarter of its size (420 ms, ease-out). Accepting, the flower opens (900 ms). Passes rise up their root to the seed as slow spores.
  • Nothing moves on its own except the spores, which are the live flow of evidence into acceptance. Hover lights things; it never animates them.
  • Reduced motion: no swelling, blooming or spores. Replay moves in whole steps (a tenth of the history, or one event of a claim's life) with no tween, and switching the axis cuts instead of morphing.

At 10, 1,000 and 50,000

10 to about 100

Every knot is drawn at full size. Every claim and artifact carries its label, tied to it by a stitch. Every hypha is drawn.

About 1,000

Knots shrink to fit, and labels give way to hover. Hyphae fade to hairlines, and the hovered knot's ancestry is lit in its authors' inks.

50,000

Knots go. Each root's girth shows how busy it was. Hyphae bundle per pair of agents per band, weighted so the paths the work really takes stand out. Only settled outcomes stay as points: accepted in green, rejected in rust. Zoom brings the knots back.
